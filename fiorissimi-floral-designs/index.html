The studio

Tables, arches and front steps, designed one event at a time.

Fiorissimi Floral Designs works from a studio on Applewood Crescent in Concord and designs for weddings, events and homes across the GTA.

Every arrangement starts with a conversation about the room, the colours and the day, then the flowers are built to fit it.
