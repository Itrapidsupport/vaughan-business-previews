From the slab to the finished counter

We sit down with you first and talk through the kitchen, bathroom or commercial space, the look you want and the budget you have.

Then we cut the stone ourselves. Our shop runs CNC equipment, and we're happy to walk you through it so you can see how your slab is handled before it reaches your house.

Contractors, custom home builders, designers and homeowners keep coming back, and plenty of our clients have been with us for 15 or 20 years.

  • Consultation

    We go over the room, the material and the edge detail with you, at the showroom or on site.

  • In-house fabrication

    Kitchen and vanity countertops, tops, sills and cut-to-size slabs made on our CNC equipment.

  • Installation

    Our own installers handle the fit. Same standard on a single vanity or a full building.

  • Finishes

    Polished, honed, leathered, vintage, sandblasted, flamed and more, depending on the stone.

  • Edge profiles

    Choose the edge for your counter. We'll show you samples in the showroom.

  • Sinks and faucets

    Undermount sinks and faucets supplied and cut in with your counter.
