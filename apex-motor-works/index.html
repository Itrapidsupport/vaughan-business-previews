An owner-operated shop for European cars

Howard Prophet has worked on European cars for more than 30 years. In 2012 he opened Apex Motor Works with his wife and business partner, Maryann Rizzo.

Howard is known for his collection of diagnostic equipment and his knowledge of European cars, and for setting cars up for driver education days at the track.

Regular customers get discounts on factory original parts, and every job gets the same care, from a scheduled service to an engine rebuild.

What we do

    Warning lights, rough running and the faults other shops could not find, traced with the right equipment for your make.

  • Scheduled maintenance

  • Engine rebuilding

  • Transmissions and differentials

  • Alignment and welding

  • Body, paint and detailing
