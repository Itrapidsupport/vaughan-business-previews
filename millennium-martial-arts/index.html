The dojo

Dream it, believe it, do it.

Millennium Martial Arts opened in 1997 to offer teaching of the kind you would find in Japan, for the whole family. We have been at the same clean, comfortable location in Vaughan for almost 30 years.

Classes are small, so every student gets personal instruction and moves at their own pace. Students build self-defence skills, discipline and confidence, and leave the stress of the day on the mat.

We teach students from Vaughan, Woodbridge, Maple, Thornhill and Richmond Hill.

What we teach

One monthly fee covers unlimited classes in any or all of our styles, so you can fit training around your week.

  1. 01 / 06

    Kids

    Classes for ages 5 to 12. Children learn to defend themselves against a larger, stronger attacker, and build balance, focus, confidence and respect along the way.

    • Break-falls and rolls
    • Stances, punches, kicks
    • Wooden sword and staff
    • Partner techniques
    • Meditation
    • Belt evaluations

  2. 02 / 06

    Aikido

    Yoshinkan Aikido, the style taught by Gozo Shioda. The soft art of self-defence that uses an attacker's own power against them, practised with a partner and with the jo, bokken and tanto.

    • Partner techniques
    • Multiple attackers
    • Rolls and break-falls
    • Jo, bokken, tanto
    • Timing and focus
    • Meditation

  3. 03 / 06

    Jujitsu

    Yoshin Ryu Jujitsu, a stand-up street self-defence style with no ground fighting. Students learn to use leverage rather than force to deal with a bigger attacker, armed or not.

    • Throws and take-downs
    • Locks and holds
    • Pressure points
    • Kicks, punches, blocks
    • Sparring
    • Weapons defence

  4. 04 / 06

    Karate

    Goju Ryu Karate from Okinawa, the art of hard and soft. Preparatory exercises, katas, kumite with a partner and sparring, for adults and children.

    • Kicks and punches
    • Blocks and katas
    • Kumite
    • Sparring
    • Cardio and conditioning
    • Stress relief

  5. 05 / 06

    Tai Chi

    Yang style Tai Chi Chuan, practised as a slow, meditative exercise for balance, flexibility, breathing and a calm mind, rather than for fighting.

    • Yang style long form
    • Simple form
    • Deep breathing
    • Balance and posture

  6. 06 / 06

    Weapons

    Traditional weapons are part of our Aikido, Jujitsu and Karate classes, along with how to defend against knives, sticks and other weapons.

    • Long, medium, short staff
    • Wooden sword
    • Nunchaku
    • Sai
    • Tonfa
    • Kama
