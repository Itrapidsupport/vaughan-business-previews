The family shop

The sons of a butcher, running the shop their own way since 2007.

Michael and Sam Totera learned the trade in their father's butcher shop, which he started in the 1970s. They began with store maintenance and worked their way up to the block, then opened their own retail and wholesale business in Maple.

Many customers have been coming since the first year. Expect a friendly greeting, an espresso, and a few cooking tips with whatever you take home.
