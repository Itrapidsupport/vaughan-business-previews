A neighbourhood place in Maple

Farro is Roberto DeLuca and Carmine Biasi's restaurant. Chef Roberto runs the kitchen, and the menu mixes the Italian classics people come back for with a few newer ideas.

Come in for a quick lunch or settle in for a long dinner. Pizza and pasta can be made with spelt, whole wheat or gluten-free dough, and there's always something for the kids.

The wine list runs past a hundred bottles, mostly Italian, and you're welcome to bring your own.

  • Wood-burning oven
  • Patio

  • Gluten-free options
  • Take-out

Every group menu can be changed to suit you, and we can bring the food to you with our catering.
