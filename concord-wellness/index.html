What we offer

All our massage therapists are registered with the College of Massage Therapists of Ontario. We also have an acupuncturist, a manual osteopath and a chiropractor on staff.

Massage therapy

For back, neck and shoulder pain, sprains, strains and tendonitis, recovery after an injury, or just to let the stress go.

  • Swedish and relaxation massage
  • Deep tissue massage
  • Sports massage
  • Hot stone massage
  • Myofascial release
  • Reflexology

Acupuncture

Often used for headaches, chronic pain, tendinitis and arthritis. Some people feel a change after one or two visits, others need a few weeks.

Osteopathy

Hands-on treatment from a certified manual osteopath for back and neck pain and posture problems from driving or desk work. It may be covered by your extended health plan.

Chiropractic care

Spinal adjustments, corrective exercises you can do at home, and advice on the activities that keep aggravating the problem.

FACEFIT facial massage

A non-invasive facial massage that works along the vertical lines of the face to tone the muscles and skin.

Medical supplies

We're an authorized Sigvaris dealer and also carry orthotics and braces.

  • Compression stockings
  • Orthotics
  • Braces

Before your visit

  • On your first visit, please come 5 to 10 minutes early to fill in a short health history form.
  • Tell your therapist about any allergies, medical conditions or medication.
  • We need 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. Missed appointments are charged 20% of the treatment fee.
  • Check with your benefits provider whether osteopathy and acupuncture are covered on your plan.
